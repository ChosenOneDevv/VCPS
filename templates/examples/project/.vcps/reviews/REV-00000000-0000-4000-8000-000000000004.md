## Review: sync-conflict resolution

**Intended behavior** (reconstructed from `INV-001` and `src/sync.js`'s
own header comment): on a same-second write collision between two
devices, the merge SHOULD prefer whichever write has `done: true`,
never silently prefer an older incomplete state.

**Actual behavior found:** the tiebreak used device-registration order,
not completion state -- exactly the gap `INV-001` was registered to
catch. See `FND-00000000-0000-4000-8000-00000000000c` for the full
finding and `BUG-00000000-0000-4000-8000-000000000005` for the fix.

No other deviation was found in this review; the remainder of
`src/sync.js`'s merge logic matches its documented intent.

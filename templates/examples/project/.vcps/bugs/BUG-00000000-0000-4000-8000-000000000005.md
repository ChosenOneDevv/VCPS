## Fix: sync timestamp tiebreak

**Symptom:** a completed todo item occasionally reappeared as incomplete
after syncing between two devices.

**Reproduction:** two devices submit a sync write within the same
wall-clock second, one with `done: true` (just completed) and one with
`done: false` (hadn't yet observed the completion). The old tiebreak
picked whichever device registered first, independent of `done` state.

**Root cause:** `src/sync.js`'s merge function's same-timestamp tiebreak
used device-registration order instead of preferring `done: true`.

**Fix:** tiebreak now always prefers a write with `done: true` when two
writes share a timestamp.

**Regression:** `EVD-00000000-0000-4000-8000-00000000000d`,
`tests/sync.test.js::concurrent_sync_timestamp_tie_keeps_done_state`.

**Affected revisions:** the defect existed from `todo-cli`'s original
implementation through revision `7b2e5a814f33` (where it was found);
fixed at `c49d0f6a1e88`.

## Recurring todos recompute in UTC, not per-user local time

`FEAT-00000000-0000-4000-8000-000000000003` (recurring todo items)
schedules the next occurrence at UTC midnight rather than the
household's local midnight. For a household near a timezone boundary,
a daily recurring item can appear to repeat slightly early or late
relative to their own calendar day.

This is a deliberate, documented, low-impact limitation accepted by the
project owner rather than a defect: it does not affect `INV-001` or any
other registered invariant, and the household workaround (reschedule
the one affected occurrence manually) is cheap. A future `FEATURE` MAY
add per-user timezone support.

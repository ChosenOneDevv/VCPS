## Sync timestamp-tiebreak defect

`src/sync.js`'s merge function resolved same-second write collisions by
device-registration order rather than by data semantics. A device that
had not yet observed a completion could "win" a tie and overwrite the
completed state with its own stale `done: false`, which is exactly the
behavior `INV-001` ("A todo item marked done is never silently reverted
to not-done by a background sync") exists to prevent.

Fixed and verified under `BUG-00000000-0000-4000-8000-000000000005`;
see `GATE-00000000-0000-4000-8000-000000000008` for the Gate 4
evaluation and `EVD-00000000-0000-4000-8000-00000000000d` for the
regression evidence.
